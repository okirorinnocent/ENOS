Safety and good practice

Always read the label. Use products only as directed and follow Ugandan regulations.

Protect yourself

Wear gloves, boots, long sleeves, a mask and goggles. Do not eat, drink or smoke while spraying. Wash hands and clothes afterwards.

Store and dispose

Keep chemicals in original containers, locked, away from food, children and animals. Triple-rinse empty containers, pour rinse water into the tank, then puncture and bury them. Never reuse them.

Before harvest

Respect the pre-harvest interval (PHI) on the label. Do not spray near water sources or when it is windy or about to rain.

First aid

Move the person to fresh air, remove contaminated clothing, rinse skin or eyes with clean water for 15 minutes. Do not induce vomiting. Go to the nearest health centre at once and carry the product label.

Farmer tips

How to read a pesticide label

Check the active ingredient, target pest, crop, dose, PHI, warning colour band and first aid before you buy or mix.

Fall armyworm: scout early

Check young maize weekly for window-pane holes and frass in the whorl. Early, targeted control works better than late heavy spraying.

Calibrate your sprayer

Spray a measured plot with plain water first to learn how many tanks per acre you really use. It saves money and avoids overdosing.
